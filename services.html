Services

Two disciplines, run as one engagement.

We don't hand you a journey map and walk away, and we don't sell automation for its own sake. Each engagement moves from experience insight to a funded, sequenced strategy for growth or efficiency.

Service 01

Customer Experience Journey Mapping & Business Process Mapping Workshops

A facilitated, design-thinking workshop series that maps what your customers actually experience against the business process delivering it — then uses that shared picture to brainstorm new experiences your teams can prototype and test.

  • ✓End-to-end journey maps built from real customer and frontline input, not assumptions from a conference room.
  • ✓Parallel process maps showing the internal handoffs, systems, and decisions behind each journey stage.

How the Workshop Runs

From current state to tested concept.

A typical engagement runs four phases over three to six weeks, scaled to the complexity of the journey in scope.

Phase 1

Discover

Stakeholder interviews, frontline shadowing, and data review to understand the journey and process as they actually operate today — not as they're documented.

Phase 2

Map

Facilitated workshops build the current-state journey map and process map together, pinpointing moments of friction, delight, cost, and risk.

Phase 3

Ideate

Design-thinking brainstorm sessions generate future-state experience concepts, which we cluster, sketch, and prioritize against feasibility and impact.

Phase 4

Prototype & Validate

Selected concepts are prototyped at low fidelity and tested with real customers or users, producing evidence for what to build next.
