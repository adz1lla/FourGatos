Industries

Five industries. Both sides of the transaction.

We work with executives running both B2B and B2C operations across Retail, Financial Services, Manufacturing, CPG, and High Tech — wherever a complex journey meets a complex process.

01  ·  B2B & B2C

Retail

From storefront to fulfillment, retail journeys span more touchpoints and handoffs than almost any other category. We map the path across channels — in-store, ecommerce, marketplace, and wholesale — and pair it with the fulfillment, merchandising, and service processes behind it, then identify where automation can absorb volume without eroding the experience.
